-- Local v7 pronunciation assets --

The v7 local preview contains pre-generated, independent MP3 pronunciation
clips for offline playback. British clips use en-GB-SoniaNeural and American
clips use en-US-JennyNeural. Playback does not call the device speech engine
and the application contains no runtime API key or personal credential.
